Every zoned senior high in Miami-Dade, mapped and color-coded by region — North, Central, and South. Click a pin or a school in the list to see attendance-zone details.

Mapped from the Miami-Dade County Public Schools "High Attendance Boundary" record. Note: the uploaded export contained the school directory (name, address, region) but not the underlying attendance-zone polygon geometry — spreadsheet exports of a geodatabase layer drop the shape field. Pins mark each school's campus location; region colors (N / C / S) reflect the official zone assignment. For the exact zone polygons, see Miami-Dade County's Open Data Hub.
